# v2.1 Governed Project Multiplier Rolling-Origin Validation

**Status:** isolated shadow validation only. No production evaluator, official Accuracy artifact, calibration version, weights, fallback policy, raw DLD source, or historical result was changed.

## Implemented proposal

This experiment applies the proposed safeguards to the project signal. Each project is compared with a time-normalized, area/room-matched peer cohort within the same property type and district. The raw project signal is partially pooled toward the neutral value using the lower of the project sample weight and peer support weight:

> `Reliability = min(Project Sample Weight, Peer Support Weight)`

> `Pooled Signal = 1 + Reliability × (Relative Project Signal − 1)`

The experiment uses a 5% neutral band, excludes project or peer cohorts with a `P75/P25` dispersion ratio above `2`, requires at least eight project transactions and five peer transactions, and bounds the final direct multiplier to `0.98×–1.02×`. Records without a qualifying cohort fall back to the official baseline unchanged.

## Stability of direct multipliers

| Transition | Common cohorts | Median absolute change | P90 absolute change | Same sign | Exact same multiplier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| origin-1 → origin-2 | 339 | 0.000× | 0.020× | 82.301% | 78.761% |
| origin-2 → origin-3 | 421 | 0.000× | 0.020× | 86.223% | 83.610% |
| origin-3 → origin-4 | 495 | 0.000× | 0.020× | 85.859% | 83.838% |

Among 499 project cohorts seen in at least two origins, 72.345% remained within a total multiplier range of `0.01×` or less. This is an improvement over the earlier wider-bound experiment, where 62.124% met that stability threshold. However, the remaining projects can still move across the full `0.98×–1.02×` range. The narrower bound improves stability partly by limiting impact, not by proving that the underlying project signal is correct.

## Decision

The proposed controls work as safeguards: they reduce coefficient volatility, increase the exact-repeat rate between origins, and prevent large price movements. They do not yet produce a consistent accuracy improvement. Because MAE is higher in every origin and Apartment performance is consistently worse, the governed estimator should remain shadow-only and disabled by default.

The next safe improvement is not to widen the multiplier or force the project adjustment. It is to test a hierarchical residual model with a pre-declared rolling validation schedule, a project-specific reliability threshold, and a neutral fallback that can apply by property type. Any candidate activation should require improvement in at least two primary metrics without worsening MAE across the principal property types, plus adequate coverage and stable sign across origins.

BUA/Plot Area and Last Renovation Year remain user-supplied scenario factors. The current DLD files do not provide verified labels for them, so this rolling-origin artifact does not fit or claim coefficients for those fields.

## Reproducibility

The runner is `scripts/rolling-origin-v21-governed-project-validation.js`, and the machine-readable output is `docs/v2.1-governed-project-rolling-origin.json`. All inputs are existing local DLD and verified Accuracy artifacts. The experiment does not write to official data or calibration storage.
